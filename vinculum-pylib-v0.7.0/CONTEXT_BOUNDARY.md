# Origin, representation and retreat

VINCULUM's measurable domain begins at representations: words, numeric quantities, defined attributes, observations and models.

The optional `NarrativeContext.deep_sigma()` carries the user's origin sequence from God through physical/living reality into representation, and the retreat sequence from names/numbers/divisions through human experience and nature toward stillness before God.

This context is deliberately attributed and unscored. It does not enter a PairGraph as a representation, influence a collision indicator or establish a scientific cosmology. Changing the context cannot change the numerical report. It may appear in the offline report viewer and scenario metadata.

The implementation therefore accommodates both approved visuals without turning a worldview into an algorithmic claim or scoring a relationship with God.

**In work, refine the descriptions. In retreat, set down the need to describe.**
