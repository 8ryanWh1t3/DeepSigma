# Versioning Policy

DOO uses semantic versioning for the ontology package.

- **PATCH**: annotations, examples, documentation, validation fixes that do not intentionally change term semantics.
- **MINOR**: additive classes/properties/profiles or backward-compatible semantic extensions.
- **MAJOR**: removals, renamed canonical terms, changed domain/range commitments, or meaning changes that can alter inference or round-trip behavior.

Deprecated terms remain mapped for at least one major-version transition when practical.
