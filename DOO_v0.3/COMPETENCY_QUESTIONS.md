# Competency Questions

A DOO implementation should be able to answer the questions appropriate to its profile:

1. What decision was made?
2. Which problem did it address?
3. Who or what made it?
4. When was it made?
5. Why was it made?
6. Which alternatives were considered, selected, rejected, or deferred?
7. Which criteria, risks, tradeoffs, and dependencies mattered?
8. Which claims supported the decision?
9. Which evidence supported or challenged those claims?
10. Which assumptions were load-bearing, and are they still valid?
11. What authority and approval were represented at decision time?
12. What actions and outcomes followed?
13. What reviews, contradictions, or drift signals occurred?
14. What patch or successor decision changed the original?
15. Can the decision episode be exported with lineage and reconstructed elsewhere?
