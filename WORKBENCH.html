Three records. Two connections. One small example of what an ontology does.

Start with three ordinary records.

A decision refers to a claim. The claim refers to an inspection report. The IDs let us link the records without mixing them up.

The ontology tells us what each record means.

Decision is a type of thing. D-01 is one specific decision. The other records work the same way.

Types and definitions come from the included Coherence Ops ontology. The three records are invented only for this walkthrough.
